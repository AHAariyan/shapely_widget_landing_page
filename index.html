Questions

Will it work with my phone's home screen?

Yes. Shapely uses Android's standard widgets, so they work with your phone's own launcher and most others. Add them from the app, or from your home screen's widget list.

Does it work on tablets?

Yes. Every widget is drawn at its exact size, so it stays sharp on big screens and when you resize it.

Will widgets drain my battery?

No. Clocks are kept on time by Android itself, and the other widgets only update when something changes, like the weather or your battery level.

Is Pro a subscription?

No. It's a single payment that unlocks everything, including widgets we add later.

Can I change a widget after I add it?

Any time. Open Shapely, change the colours, shape, font or photo, and it updates on your home screen.
